# Rockland Streamer University: paste-ready copy

Written in Little Rock's voice, never Naomi's. Nothing here is self-promotion in her name.

## NewsWatch story (LRTVS NewsWatch, 180 words)

**Little Rock opens a streaming school in Berbice**

Little Rock is opening Rockland Streamer University, a six-week school that teaches people in Berbice to broadcast live from a phone or a laptop and then puts them on air on Little Rock's own network.

The first cohort is free and has twenty seats. Enrolment opens Monday 19 October at lrtvs.gy/university. Classes begin Saturday 7 November at 10:00 in the Pink Room, Little Rock's studio, and every live class also streams on Channel 10 live at lrtvs.gy/live.

Students pick one of five tracks: gamers, hosts, live sellers, citizen reporters and event streamers. The twelve lessons cover going live from a phone, clean audio, OBS, channel branding, hosting, games, music and copyright law, money, safety for minors, and growth. Every assignment is a real stream.

Graduates stream a one-hour show on Saturday 19 December, receive a numbered certificate, join the Rockland Streamer U Twitch team, and get first call for guest slots on 88.5 Rock FM and Channel 10.

Students aged 13 to 17 enrol with a guardian. Cohort 1 is sponsored by Little Rock's advertisers.

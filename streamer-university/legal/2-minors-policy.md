# Rockland Streamer University: policy for students aged 13 to 17 (DRAFT for Naomi's review)

1. Students must be 13 or over on the first day of class. No student under 13 is enrolled, because the platforms do not allow it.
2. A student under 18 enrols with a guardian who signs the enrolment agreement, this policy and the media release.
3. The guardian attends class 1 in person and is reachable by phone during every live class and every assignment stream.
4. On stream, a student under 18 never shows or says: home address, school name, daily route, phone number, or a view that places the home. Other children do not appear on the student's stream.
5. The student's platform accounts are created truthfully with the student's real date of birth, with the guardian's knowledge, and with two-factor authentication on. The guardian holds a recovery method.
6. Direct messages from strangers are off or set to friends only. Offers of money, gifts or sponsorship sent to a minor are shown to the guardian and to the school before any reply.
7. A guardian or the school may stop any stream at any time.
8. The school's staff never contact a student under 18 one to one by private message; contact goes through the cohort group or the guardian's number.
9. A breach of this policy ends the enrolment.

# Rockland Streamer University: enrolment agreement and code of conduct (DRAFT for Naomi's review)

Between Little Rock ("the school") and the student named below ("the student"), and the student's guardian where the student is under 18.

1. **The course.** Six weeks, twelve lessons, from 7 November to 19 December 2026. Six live Saturday classes at the Pink Room, Berbice, 10:00 to 12:00, also streamed on Channel 10 live. Six self-paced lessons at lrtvs.gy/university. Twelve assignments, each a live stream by the student on the student's own channel.
2. **Fee.** Cohort 1 is free. The student pays nothing to the school. The student's own equipment, internet and platform accounts are the student's responsibility.
3. **Attendance.** A student who misses two live classes without notice gives up the seat to the waiting list. Assignments are due before the next live class.
4. **Graduation.** A student who completes the twelve assignments and streams the one-hour graduation show on 19 December receives a numbered certificate, a listing on the graduates wall, and a place on the Rockland Streamer U Twitch team. The school decides completion in good faith.
5. **Code of conduct.** On every channel used for the course and at the Pink Room: family-friendly content at all times; no gore, no sexual content, no hate, no harassment, no gambling promotion; no illegal content; no music the student has not cleared (see the music policy); no doxxing of anyone; respect for staff, sponsors and other students. The school may remove a student from the course for a breach, with no refund (there is no fee) and no liability.
6. **Accounts.** Channels belong to the student. The school never asks for a password or a stream key and the student never shares one. Platform rules (age, country, conduct) are the student's to meet; the platform policy sheet explains them.
7. **Privacy.** The school keeps the student's name, age bracket, guardian name, town, track, contact number and application answers on its own server for the course and for one year after, to contact the student about the school and alumni opportunities. It does not publish or sell them. The student may ask for the record to be deleted after graduation.
8. **Media.** Covered by the separate media release.
9. **Safety.** The school is not responsible for the student's conduct or safety on the student's own streams outside class. The safety lesson sets out the school's recommendations.
10. **Liability.** To the extent the law allows, the school is not liable for loss arising from the course, platform action against a channel, equipment loss, or internet failure.

# Legal drafts for Rockland Streamer University

Four skeleton documents for Naomi to review and finish. They are drafts by an assistant, not legal advice, and nothing is issued until she signs it off. Guyana law governs; she decides the governing-law and dispute clauses.

| File | What | Who signs |
|------|------|-----------|
| `1-enrolment-agreement.md` | enrolment terms and code of conduct | every student; guardian too if under 18 |
| `2-minors-policy.md` | the rules for students aged 13 to 17 | guardian |
| `3-media-release.md` | permission for Little Rock to rebroadcast class and graduation streams | every student; guardian if under 18 |
| `4-music-and-platform-policy.md` | the cleared-music rule and the platform age and country rules, stated plainly | read and initialled |

Privacy note carried in every document: the enrolment list lives on Little Rock's server only, is never published, never committed to a repository and never pasted into a chat.

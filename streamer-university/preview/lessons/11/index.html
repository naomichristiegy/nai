Clips, VODs and growth

One stream becomes ten posts. Clipping, shorts, titles, thumbnails and a posting rhythm that fits around a job.

A stream is raw material. The VOD is the record; the clips are the advertising. Three clips per stream, 15 to 60 seconds, posted as TikToks, Reels and Shorts the next day.

A clip needs a first second that explains itself. Trim until the moment starts immediately. Add captions; most phones watch muted.

Titles say what happens, not what you are: 'Lost the final with one HP' beats 'Stream highlights'. Thumbnails: a face, big text, three words, your two colours.

Rhythm: stream twice a week, post three clips a day after each stream, one post on the off days. That is ten posts from two streams. Batch the editing into one hour.

Measure one number: average viewers on Twitch, watch time on YouTube, views on TikTok. Look at it weekly, not hourly. Growth is boring and slow and then sudden.

Do it

  1. Watch your last VOD and mark three moments.
  2. Clip them to under 60 seconds each with captions.
  3. Write a title that says what happens.
  4. Post one to TikTok, one to Shorts, one to Reels.
  5. Write your weekly rhythm in the bio.

Assignment

Three clips from your own VOD posted on three platforms. Links in the group.

Before you post it

Lesson 12 · Week 6 · Graduation, Saturday 19 December

The graduation stream

A one-hour show on the Rockland Streamer University stage, on your own channel and the Streamer U team, judged live.

Your graduation project is a one-hour stream in your track: a game session, a talk show, a live sale, a reporting hit, or an event. Announced a week before. On schedule.

The stage: your channel is added to the Rockland Streamer University Twitch team for the day, Channel 10 live carries highlights, and 88.5 reads the lineup on air. Family in Berbice and the diaspora can watch from lrtvs.gy.

Judging, live, by Naomi and two graduates or guests: audio, hook, chat handling, brand, safety, and whether the hour had a shape (a start, a middle, an end).

You graduate with a numbered certificate, a place on the team, a listing on the graduates wall, and first call for guest slots on 88.5 and Channel 10.

Then you keep the schedule you set in lesson 6. The school was six weeks. The channel is yours for as long as you show up.

Do it

  1. Pick the graduation show in your track and announce it a week ahead.
  2. Rehearse the hook and the ending.
  3. Check audio, scenes, moderation and the room the day before.
  4. Go live at your slot. One hour. Read chat by name.
  5. Say your schedule, thank the room, end on time.

Lesson 6 · Week 3 · Self-paced

Talk to nobody, then to everybody

Hosting an empty room without dying inside, reading chat, the hook, and the schedule that makes people come back.

Every streamer started with zero viewers. Talk anyway. The recording becomes clips; the habit becomes the show. Pretend one friend is watching and talk to them.

The hook is the first 30 seconds: what is happening today, why it matters, what you are about to do. Say it again every ten minutes for the people who just arrived.

Reading chat: say the person's name, read the message, answer it. Nothing grows a channel faster than being seen. Never read out anything private someone types; say it was received.

Silence is fine for ten seconds. Longer than that, narrate what you are doing. Describe, react, ask the room a question.

A schedule beats talent. Same day, same time, every week, and say it at the end of every stream. Put it in the bio. People plan around it.

Do it

  1. Write your 30-second hook on paper. Say it three times.
  2. Pick a weekly day and time and put it in your bio.
  3. Go live at that exact time with the Starting Soon scene for three minutes first.
  4. Stream 30 minutes. Read every message by name.
  5. End with the schedule line.

Assignment

A 30-minute stream at a pre-announced time. Post the announcement and the VOD link.

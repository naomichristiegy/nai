Money

How a streamer in Guyana actually gets paid, platform by platform, and the one-page revenue plan every graduate leaves with.

Twitch Affiliate: 50 followers, 7 stream days in 30, 3 average viewers. Unlocks subscriptions and Bits. Payout by PayPal or bank transfer, minimum US$50. Partner comes later and is not needed to earn.

YouTube Partner: 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours, or 10 million Shorts views. Ads, memberships, Super Chat. Payout needs a verified AdSense account.

TikTok LIVE gifts: viewers send gifts, you cash out diamonds. Available in some countries only; check your account's eligibility before planning around it.

Sponsors and selling: the money that does not depend on a platform. A local business pays for a mention; a seller streams the stall and takes orders in chat; a link in bio goes to a Shopify page. Little Rock's advertiser list is a template for how to price a mention.

Taxes and records: income is income. Keep a simple sheet of every payout. Platforms ask for tax forms; fill them truthfully. Never let anyone else's account receive your money.

Do it

  1. Check which monetisation your platforms allow for your account today.
  2. Write what a mention on your stream would cost, in GY$.
  3. List three local businesses who could sponsor you.
  4. Set up the payout method on one platform.
  5. Fill the one-page revenue plan template from the group.

Assignment

A one-page revenue plan: platform money, sponsor money, selling money, and the first target date for each.

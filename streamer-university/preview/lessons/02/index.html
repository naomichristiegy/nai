Your channel, your name

Pick a handle you will not regret, set up two channels properly, and lock them so nobody takes them from you.

A handle is a name people type. Short, spellable when heard on radio, the same on every platform. Check it is free on Twitch, YouTube, TikTok and Instagram before you commit. Avoid numbers you will have to explain.

Profile picture: your face or a simple mark, readable at the size of a fingernail. Banner: your name, what you stream, when you stream. No phone numbers, no home address, no school name.

Bio: one sentence of what the channel is, one of when you are live, one link. Under 150 characters so it shows without a tap.

Settings that matter on day one: date of birth truthfully, two-factor authentication on, email verified, a recovery phone. Country is whatever is true for you.

Twitch needs a stream key to go live from a computer. That key is a password. You will never read it aloud, never type it in a chat, never show it on screen.

Do it

  1. Check the handle on four platforms.
  2. Create or rename the channel on Twitch and one other platform.
  3. Upload a profile picture and a banner.
  4. Write the bio.
  5. Turn on two-factor and verify the email.

Assignment

Two channels set up with picture, banner, bio and two-factor on. Post the two links in the cohort group.

Before you post it

Ticks stay on this phone only.

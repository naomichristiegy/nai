House rules

  • Family-friendly, on every channel in this school. Pink paint, never gore.
  • Students 13 to 17 enrol with a guardian who signs and sits in on the first live.
  • Nothing private on stream: no addresses, schools, plates, documents.
  • Cleared music only. The 88.5 Rock FM stream is cleared for every student.
  • Stream keys are passwords. Never read aloud, never typed in chat, never on screen.

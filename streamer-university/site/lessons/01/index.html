Go live from a phone today

Your first stream happens in this lesson, from the phone in your hand, in a Berbice room with Berbice light.

Every platform you already use can go live: TikTok (1,000 followers needed, or use a friend's account to practise), Instagram, Facebook, YouTube (50 subscribers needed for mobile live). Pick the one where your people already are.

Light: face the window, never sit with it behind you. Afternoon sun through a Berbice window is the best free light in the world. At night, one lamp in front of you and slightly above, never the ceiling bulb alone.

Framing: phone upright for TikTok and Instagram, sideways for YouTube and Facebook. Eyes one third from the top of the screen. Camera at eye level, so stack books under the phone if you have no tripod.

Sound: the phone mic is fine if the room is quiet and you are within one metre. Fans off. Dogs out. A towel on the table kills echo.

The first ten seconds: say who you are, where you are, and what is about to happen. People decide to stay in ten seconds.

Do it

  1. Charge the phone to 100% and turn on Do Not Disturb.
  2. Open the platform, tap Live, write a title that says what you will do.
  3. Check the picture, check the light, say the ten-second opener.
  4. Stream for two minutes. Read every comment out loud.
  5. Say goodbye, say when you are back, end the live.

Assignment

A two-minute live on any platform. Screenshot the ended-live screen (viewers, time) and post it in the cohort group.

Before you post it

Ticks stay on this phone only.

Safety and minors

Doxxing, location leaks, kids on camera, moderation settings, and the moment you stop a stream.

Never on stream: your address, your school, your workplace schedule, a view out the window that places your house, documents on the desk, a package label, a car number plate.

Kids: platforms require 13 and up, with guardian involvement under 18. In this school, students under 18 stream with a guardian who has signed and who is present for the first live. Other children, yours or anyone's, stay off camera unless their guardian has agreed in writing.

Moderation: AutoMod on, level 2 or higher. Followers-only chat if trolls arrive. Name one moderator before your first public stream and give them the power to time out and ban. Block and move on; never argue.

Private messages: anyone offering money, sponsorships or gifts by DM wants something. Verified brand emails only, and ask the cohort group before answering.

Stop the stream when: someone in chat posts your private information, a child walks on camera, you feel unsafe, or you are too tired to think. Ending early costs nothing. Say you will be back and end it.

Do it

  1. Walk through your streaming room on camera and remove everything that places or names you.
  2. Turn on AutoMod and set the level.
  3. Appoint a moderator and tell them the rules.
  4. Set DMs to friends only on every platform.
  5. Write the three things that would make you end a stream.

Assignment

Screenshot of your moderation settings and the name of your moderator posted in the group.

Before you post it

Ticks stay on this phone only.

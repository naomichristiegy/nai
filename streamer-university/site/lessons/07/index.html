Games on stream

Capturing a PC game, a console, or a browser game, with your face on it and your frame rate intact.

Game Capture source in OBS for PC games, set to capture any fullscreen application. Window Capture for browser games such as the PINKLAND games at pinkland.gy, which are free to stream and made in Berbice.

Consoles need a capture card (US$60 and up) between the console and the TV. Or stream straight from the console's own Twitch app and skip OBS until you can afford the card.

Frame rate: the game and the stream share the graphics card. On a laptop, cap the game at 60 fps, set the stream at 720p30 or 1080p30, and use NVENC so the stream encodes on the card's own chip.

Fortnite settings for streaming: Fullscreen, frame limit at the screen refresh rate, DLSS Quality, Nanite and Lumen off, hardware ray tracing off. Looks fine, runs steady.

Content rule for this school: family-friendly. Pink paint, never gore. No voice chat from strangers on stream; disable party chat capture or mute it. You are responsible for every word on your channel.

Do it

  1. Add a Game Capture or Window Capture source to Live.
  2. Add the webcam as a small circle in a corner.
  3. Cap the game frame rate and turn ray tracing off.
  4. Stream 20 minutes of a game; watch Stats for dropped frames.
  5. Clip one good moment.

Assignment

A game stream with a webcam in the corner. Post the VOD and a 30-second clip.

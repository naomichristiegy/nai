Lesson 3 · Week 2 · Live class, Saturday

Audio first

Viewers forgive a blurry picture and never forgive bad sound. This class is about hearing yourself the way they hear you.

The microphone should be close to your mouth, 10 to 20 cm. Distance is the enemy; every doubling of distance halves the voice and doubles the room.

Cheapest big upgrade: a wired lavalier mic that clips to your shirt, US$15 to 25, plugs into the phone or the laptop. Second cheapest: any gaming headset. A ring light with a built-in mic is not a microphone.

Room: soft things absorb echo. Curtains, a bed, a wardrobe behind you. A bare tiled room sounds like a bathroom.

Levels: your voice should peak at about three quarters of the meter and never hit the red. Game or music under your voice, never over it. Set it once and leave it.

Monitor yourself: record 30 seconds, play it back with headphones, listen for hiss, hum, fan, echo, and the fridge. Fix one thing at a time.

Do it

  1. Record 30 seconds on the phone and listen with headphones.
  2. Move the mic closer, record again, compare.
  3. Kill the loudest background noise you can find.
  4. Set the voice level to three quarters of the meter.
  5. Record a final 30 seconds and keep it.

Assignment

A five-minute live where at least one viewer says in chat that the audio is clean. Screenshot that message.
